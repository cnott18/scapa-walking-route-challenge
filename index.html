How It Works

Tap the street segments that make up the route you walked to the conference. Selected streets will be highlighted.

Tap a highlighted street again if you selected it by mistake. When your route is complete, enter your name and tap Submit Route.

Your selections will help SCAPA visualize how conference attendees moved through downtown Columbia.
